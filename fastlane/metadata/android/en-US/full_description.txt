<p>Behroozuac V2ray is an Android TV build based on v2rayNG, packaged as com.behroozuac.v2raytv and optimized for TV remote controls.</p>

<h3>Telegram Channel</h3>

<h3>Main Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>Android TV dark/neon UI with larger rows and focus states.</li>
<li>Connect button tests all imported configs with real ping and connects to the lowest-delay config.</li>
<li>Clicking a config connects that exact config.</li>
<li>Add configs by phone: scan the TV QR code with a phone, paste configs into the local web page, and send them to the Android TV app.</li>
<li>No built-in proxy configs in the public build.</li>
</ul>
